We hire for longevity

At Infinity, we care deeply about our patients, our team and the impact we get to make every day. We are a faith-based center, and our faith shapes the way we care for our patients, serve one another and approach our work.

We're looking for self-starters who are highly motivated and excited to keep growing: people who take initiative, care about doing things well, support the people around them and adapt as we grow.

We aren't looking for people who want to work here for a year or two before moving on. We're looking for people who want to become part of something, take on greater responsibility and grow with us over time.

Five things we ask of everyone

Carrying each other's crosses

We step in with compassion to support patients, families and teammates, lightening their load whenever we can.

Life-changing impact

Every action we take is focused on creating meaningful, lasting progress for the patients and families we serve.

Growth mindset

We are committed to continual learning and development, as individuals and as a team, so we can better serve others.

Simplicity with excellence

We keep processes clear and effective, focusing on what truly matters so families experience care without confusion.

Adaptability

We embrace change with flexibility and creativity, always seeking the best way forward for our patients and our team.

months of training at our Iowa campus

We invest heavily in our people

For doctors we hire, our ideal training pathway includes an extended program at our Iowa campus for up to six months, so new doctors are immersed in our systems, philosophy, clinical approach and culture before stepping fully into their long-term role.

This isn't a hard requirement. It's highly valued, but we understand circumstances vary.

Before you apply

We want to get to know you, not just your resume. Here's what we'll ask for.

Step 1

Your resume

Your experience, education and skills. PDF or Word, whichever you have.

Step 2

A cover letter

Why you're interested in Infinity, and what you'd bring to our team.

Step 3

A short video

A quick introduction answering one question: why do you want to work at Infinity? Paste the link; don't worry about making it perfect.

Step 4

A short questionnaire

Nineteen multiple-choice questions about how you work, what motivates you and how you approach growth and teamwork.
