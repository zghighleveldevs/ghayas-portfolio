"We approach everything from a developmental standpoint."

Dr. Josh Madsen

Our philosophy is rooted in the understanding that learning, language and behavior are intimately connected to the motor system and movement control. Most education and many remedial techniques aim to teach higher centers in the brain.

A neuro-motor developmental approach instead identifies the lowest or earliest level of dysfunction and targets therapy there, starting from where the child is and building connections upwards. This "bottom-up" approach is the foundation of our practice.

Learning starts in the brain, which both receives information and expresses knowledge. By nurturing the brain's natural processes and prioritizing basic motor skills, we help children overcome challenges that may otherwise hinder their learning and behavior.
