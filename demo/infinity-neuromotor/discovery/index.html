Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Ask your Care Coordinator anything on the call.

What is a discovery call?

A free call with one of our Care Coordinators to discuss your child's needs and decide whether our holistic approach is right for your family.

Do you accept insurance?

We do not accept insurance. CareCredit financing is available for eligible families.

How much does the neurodevelopmental exam cost?

The initial exam is $550. This one-hour evaluation covers reflexes, vestibular function and ocular skills. Afterwards you receive an individualized care plan with exact recommendations and pricing.

What do intensive programs cost?

Intensive programs require a $1,000 deposit per week scheduled. Therapy sessions are $550 per hour, with a minimum of 9 hours weekly. Total cost depends on the recommended duration, typically 1 to 3 weeks.

What makes your approach different?

We use neuromotor techniques to retrain the brain-body connection without drugs or surgery, combined with chiropractic care, therapy and nutrition in one coordinated plan.

What should I bring to the first appointment?

Relevant medical records, a list of current medications and any previous therapy reports.

Do you offer virtual care?

Yes. Our virtual functional medicine program supports families from home. A Care Coordinator can explain eligibility and next steps.

Prefer to talk to a doctor first?

Book a 20-minute doctor consult for $200, which can be applied to future exams and intensives.
