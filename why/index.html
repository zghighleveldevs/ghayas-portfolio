Questions

Common questions

What makes you different from other GoHighLevel freelancers?

I plan the whole lead journey first, then build the website, ads, CRM, workflows and campaigns inside one GoHighLevel account so they work together. You get one connected system rather than separate pieces.

Do I need A2P registration?

Only if you send business texts to US numbers, because US carriers require A2P 10DLC registration. UK businesses don’t need it. If you do text US customers, I handle the registration and resubmit it free until it’s approved.

How quickly do you reply?

Within 24 hours, with plain-language updates on what’s done and what’s next.

Do you support the system after launch?

Yes. I keep tuning the system as your business and lead volume change, so it keeps working long after go-live.

Do you work with agencies as well as business owners?

Yes. I work directly with business owners and with agencies, including white-label builds for agency clients.
