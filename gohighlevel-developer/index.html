What a GoHighLevel developer does for your business

GoHighLevel can run your whole sales process: website, forms, calendars, CRM, email, SMS, ads and AI follow-up in one account. Most businesses only use a fraction of it, because setting it up properly takes time and know-how. That’s where a GoHighLevel developer comes in.

I plan your lead journey first, then build it inside your own account: pipelines that show where every deal is, workflows that reply and chase automatically, and pages and calendars that turn visitors into booked calls. Most setups handle 80–90% of follow-up on their own.

Already on GoHighLevel and it isn’t working? Every project starts with an audit, so I find what’s broken or missing and fix it inside the account you already have, rather than starting again.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a GoHighLevel developer cost?

It depends on what you need. Builds are a fixed scope and price agreed before any work starts, and ongoing support is a monthly plan with the scope agreed up front. We work it out on a free 30-minute call.

Can you fix my existing GoHighLevel account?

Yes. Every project starts with an audit of your current setup. I find what’s broken or missing and fix it inside the account you already have.

Do you do white-label GoHighLevel builds for agencies?

Yes. I build pipelines, dashboards and client sub-accounts inside agencies’ own branded GoHighLevel accounts, ready for them to resell.

How long does a GoHighLevel setup take?

Most builds go live within a few weeks, and you get a clear timeline before work starts.

Do you work with UK and US businesses?

Yes. I work with agencies and businesses across the UK, US and beyond, and set each system up for how that market works, including A2P registration for US texting.

Will I own my GoHighLevel account?

Yes. Everything is built in your own account and you keep ownership of it, your domains and your data. You add me as a user, so you never share your main login.
