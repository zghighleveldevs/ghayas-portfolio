What AI automation does for a growing business

Most leads are lost in the first few minutes: a call goes unanswered, a form sits in an inbox, or nobody has time to chase. AI automation closes that gap by replying instantly, day or night.

We set up AI that picks up calls when your team can’t and takes down details, replies to enquiries by SMS, email and WhatsApp, asks the right questions and books qualified leads straight into your calendar. Warm leads are handed to your team with everything they need.

It all runs inside your GoHighLevel account, connected to your CRM, so every conversation is tracked and nothing falls between tools.

Frequently asked questions

Will AI replace my team?

No. AI handles the first reply, the questions and the chasing, then hands warm leads to your team. Your people spend their time talking to buyers instead of chasing.

What can an AI voice agent do?

It can answer calls when your team is out or busy, take down the caller’s details and needs, and log everything in your CRM so someone can follow up.

Which businesses is AI automation right for?

Any business that gets enquiries by phone, forms or ads and struggles to reply fast: agencies, coaches, clinics, law firms, home improvement and e-commerce brands.

Do I need GoHighLevel?

We build the AI inside GoHighLevel so it’s connected to your CRM. If you don’t have an account yet, we can set one up for you.

How much does AI automation cost?

It depends on what you need. You get a fixed scope and price agreed before any work starts. We work it out on a free 30-minute call, with no obligation.
