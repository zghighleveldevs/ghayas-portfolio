        But what if…?

        “I’ve already paid someone to set up GoHighLevel and it didn’t work.”

        That’s more common than you’d think, and it’s why every project starts with an audit. I find what’s broken or missing and fix it inside the account you already have, so the money you’ve spent isn’t wasted.

        “I’m not technical.”

        You don’t need to be. You get a plain-English handover, and day to day you just move leads along with a click while the automations run in the background. As Paul put it: “I just click buttons now.”

        “Will I lose control of my account?”

        No. Everything is built in your own account, and you keep ownership of the account, your domains and your data. You add me as a user, so you never share your main login.

        “How long will it take?”

        Most builds go live within a few weeks, and you get a clear timeline before work starts. Mike’s full sales and marketing CRM was working in about two weeks.

        “Do you work with UK businesses?”

        Yes. I work with agencies and businesses across the UK, US and beyond, including Paul Broome in the UK. Calls are booked in your own time zone.

        “What will it cost?”

        It depends on what you need. Builds are a fixed scope and price agreed before work starts, and ongoing support is a monthly plan with the scope agreed up front. We work it out on the free call, with no obligation.
