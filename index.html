Ghayas Ahmed

I build full GoHighLevel systems end-to-end — funnels, pipelines, automations, workflows, campaigns, and CRM systems — for agencies and small businesses that need their lead follow-up to run without them. With 3–5 years of hands-on experience and 25+ clients across different industries, I've helped businesses go from having little to no system in place to building highly optimized setups that are capable of handling 80–90% of their lead management and follow-up automatically.

I understand how the market works, what makes leads convert, and how to turn a GHL account into a complete sales and automation engine. My experience covers everything from building high-converting funnels and sales pipelines to creating complex workflows, AI automations, appointment systems, nurturing campaigns, and end-to-end client journeys.

My focus has always been simple: fewer leads slipping through the cracks, less manual work, better follow-up, and more opportunities turning into paying customers.
