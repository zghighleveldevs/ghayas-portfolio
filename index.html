About Me

A hands-on GoHighLevel developer who builds full systems, not just pages.

Hi, I’m Ghayas, founder of ZG HighLevel Devs. I build full GoHighLevel systems end to end (funnels, pipelines, automations, workflows, campaigns and CRM setups) for agencies and small businesses that need their lead follow-up to run without them.

I understand what makes leads convert, and how to turn a GHL account into a complete sales and automation engine. My focus is simple: fewer leads slipping through the cracks, less manual work, better follow-up, and more opportunities turning into paying customers.
