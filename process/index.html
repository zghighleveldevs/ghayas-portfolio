Every stage in detail

What happens, and what you get

01

Stage 01

Audit

Find where leads leak

A short teardown of your current setup. I look at where leads come from, what happens after they enquire, and exactly where they go cold or get missed.

What happens

  • Review of your current GoHighLevel account, website, ads and tools
  • Lead journey mapped from first click to sale
  • List of leaks, gaps and quick wins
  • Clear recommendation of what to build first
You get: A written audit with the leaks and priorities
02

Stage 02

Map

Plan the whole system first

Before anything is built, the full system is planned: pipeline stages, automations, funnels, campaigns and how they connect. You approve the plan before work starts.

What happens

  • Pipeline stages and opportunity flow
  • Workflow and follow-up logic
  • Funnel and page structure
  • Scope, timeline and handover plan agreed
You get: A system map you sign off before the build
03

Stage 03

Build

Everything set up in your account

The full system is built inside your own GoHighLevel account: website and funnels, forms, calendars, pipelines, workflows, AI follow-up, email templates, ads, and A2P registration if you text US customers.

What happens

  • Funnels, websites and forms
  • Pipelines, calendars and custom fields
  • Workflows, AI follow-up and campaigns
  • A2P registration and integrations
You get: A complete, working system in your account
04

Stage 04

Launch

Tested end to end, then live

Every workflow, form and page is tested with real test leads before going live. Then it’s switched on, and you get a walkthrough of how everything works.

What happens

  • End-to-end tests with test contacts
  • SMS, email and booking checks
  • Go-live with monitoring
  • Recorded walkthrough and handover
You get: A live system and a walkthrough for your team
05

Stage 05

Support

Tuned as you grow

After launch, the system keeps improving. I watch how leads move, fix anything that breaks, and add new automations as your business and lead volume change.

What happens

  • Monitoring and fixes
  • New workflows and campaigns
  • Reporting on how leads move
  • Priority help when you need it
You get: A system that keeps getting better
