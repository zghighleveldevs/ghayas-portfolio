    100% A2P approval guarantee

    Approved, or I keep fixing and resubmitting it free.

    US carriers block or filter business texts sent from unregistered numbers. Rejections usually come from small details, like a missing opt-in line or a sample message that doesn’t match your use case. I prepare everything carriers check, submit it, and handle any rejection until your campaign is approved.

    • Business details that match your EIN and registration records
    • Campaign use case and sample messages written to carrier rules
    • Opt-in forms, privacy policy and terms set up on your site
    • Resubmissions handled at no extra cost if anything is rejected
