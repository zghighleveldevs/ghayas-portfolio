Ongoing Systems Support

Ongoing Systems Support

Reliable, ongoing GoHighLevel support for agencies and businesses that want their CRM, automations and campaigns improved over time, so everything keeps running cleanly in the background while they focus on growth.

01

Service Overview

This service line focuses on improving, extending and maintaining a GoHighLevel account you already use. Work happens inside your own account, on a regular basis, with clear updates on what’s been done and what’s next.

02

Who This Service Is For

  • Agencies managing several client sub-accounts
  • Established businesses already running GHL
  • Owners who want follow-up fixed and improved
  • Teams that need someone to keep the system healthy
03

CRM Development & Configuration

  • CRM setup review and clean-up
  • Custom fields, tags and custom values
  • Pipeline structure and stage mapping
  • Contact organisation and smart lists
04

Workflow & Automation

  • New multi-step workflows and triggers
  • Missed-call text-back and instant replies
  • Nurture and re-engagement sequences
  • Fixing and optimising existing automations
05

AI Voice & Follow-up

  • AI voice call follow-up for new leads
  • Reply-based branches for SMS and email
  • Handoff to your team when a lead is ready
  • Testing and tuning of AI responses
06

Funnels, Forms & Lead Capture

  • New landing pages and funnel steps
  • Forms, surveys and booking calendars
  • Lead routing into the right pipeline
  • Conversion tweaks on existing pages
07

Campaigns & Reporting

  • Email and SMS campaigns
  • Branded email templates
  • Review request automation
  • Pipeline dashboards and reporting
08

Troubleshooting & Maintenance

  • Fixing broken workflows and integrations
  • Account health checks
  • Help with GHL updates and new features
  • Priority fixes when something stops working
09

Engagement Models

Support can be set up in whichever way suits your workload. The exact scope is agreed before work starts.

  • Monthly support
  • Task-based work
  • Ongoing optimisation
  • Scope agreed upfront
10

Typical Engagement Process

  1. 1Consultation — talk through your goals and current setup
  2. 2Audit — review your account and list what to fix or build
  3. 3Plan & agreement — confirm scope, priorities and timeline
  4. 4Ongoing work — build, fix and improve inside your account
  5. 5Updates — regular progress reports in plain language
  6. 6Review — check results and agree the next priorities
11

Client Account Access & Responsibilities

Work is done inside your own GoHighLevel account. Adding me as a user or collaborator is preferred over sharing your main login, and you keep full ownership of everything built.

  • Add me as a user with the access the work needs
  • Share requirements, brand assets and logins for connected tools
  • Review and approve work at agreed checkpoints
  • Keep ownership of your account, domains and data
